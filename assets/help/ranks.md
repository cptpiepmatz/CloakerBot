Show off your takedown stats in a rank profile.


The profile includes your punisher, victim, and counter ranks, showing where 
you stand both in the current server and globally. 
Punisher rank is based on how many times you successfully called the 
{CloakerBot}. 
Victim rank tracks how often you got taken down, and counter rank shows how
many times you parried the bot. You’ll also see the exact counts for each of 
these actions.

A few badges add some extra flair. The pink shield means you're generally 
immune to takedowns, while the blue one marks immunity to random takedowns. 
A yellow flower shows you never initiated a takedown, and a ghost means you've 
never been targeted. 
The top counterer gets a red reverse card, the top striker holds the shovel, 
and the top victim gets the grave.

You can pass a `user` option to view someone else’s profile.

Take down your friends.


When called with a target the {CloakerBot} will search for the targeted user in 
a voice channel, connect to the same voice channel, play some 
[wulululu sounds](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Fv9A0Cs0lJ0) and kick the 
targeted user. 
If the targeted user is quick enough to enter {/counter}, the takedown will be 
sent onto the caller. 
After a successful takedown the bot will quote the cloaker from the video game
[Payday 2](https://store.steampowered.com/app/218620/PAYDAY_2/) and finally 
leave the channel.
